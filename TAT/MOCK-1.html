Thematic Apperception Test

Test Instructions & Guidelines

In this test, a series of 12 slides (11 hazy picture prompts followed by 1 blank slide) will be shown sequentially on the screen.

Each picture will appear for exactly 30 seconds. As soon as the 30 seconds observation period concludes, a bell will sound and writing time of 4 minutes will start automatically.

For each picture, write a complete, realistic story around the characters describing:

  • Past: What led to the situation or scene depicted.
  • Present: What is currently happening and what the characters are doing/thinking.
  • Future: What is the logical, constructive, and positive resolution or outcome.
  • 12th Blank Slide: On the final blank slide, create and narrate a self-imagined story highlighting positive leadership and problem-solving.
