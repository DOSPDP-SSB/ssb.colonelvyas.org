Word Association Test

Test Instructions & Guidelines

In this test, you will be shown 60 words in total, one after another on the screen. Each word will appear for exactly 15 seconds.

As soon as each word appears, write the first meaningful sentence that comes to your mind on your answer sheet. Your responses should be complete sentences reflecting spontaneous, positive, and constructive thoughts.

An extra 15 seconds break will automatically be provided after every 20 words (after Word 20 and Word 40) to turn over / change your page.

  • Spontaneity: Write your genuine first reaction. Do not write mere definitions, idioms, or grammar rules.
  • Dossier Format: Download the sample answer sheet or prepare a 3-page lined A4 sheet (20 sentences per page).
  • Completion: Attempt all 60 words without skipping or leaving blank entries.
